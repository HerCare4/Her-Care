OUR MISSION

Care Should Be a Necessity, Not a Luxury.

We believe every girl and woman deserves access to the basic hygiene products they need with dignity and care.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide essential hygiene products to girls and women in need while promoting dignity, confidence, and care within our community.

OUR PURPOSE

Making basic care more accessible.

Her Care works to provide practical support to people who may not always have easy access to essential hygiene products.

WHY IT MATTERS

Understanding Period Poverty

Period poverty is the inability to buy menstrual products, access clean and safe facilities, and receive proper menstrual education.

Her Care wants to help reduce these barriers by providing essential products and creating awareness within our community.

ESSENTIAL HEALTH NECESSITIES

Menstrual Products Matter

Menstrual products are essential health necessities that allow women to manage their periods safely, maintain hygiene, and participate in day-to-day life.
