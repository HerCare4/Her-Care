Our Story

Her Care was started by Ayani Bhakta as a community-based passion project focused on supporting girls and women who may struggle to access basic hygiene products.

I started Her Care because I believe basic hygiene products should be a necessity, not a luxury. Many girls and women experience situations where they cannot easily afford or access products they need, especially those experiencing financial difficulties or staying in shelters.

I was inspired by the idea that small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone's life. I wanted to create something that could provide practical support while also reminding girls and women that they are cared for, valued, and deserving of dignity.
