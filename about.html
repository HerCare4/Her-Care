ABOUT HER CARE

Who We Are

Her Care is a community-based passion project dedicated to making basic hygiene products more accessible to girls and women in need.

WHO STARTED HER CARE?

Her Care — by Ayani Bhakta

Her Care – by Ayani Bhakta was started by Ayani Bhakta as a community-based passion project focused on supporting girls and women who may struggle to access basic hygiene products.

OUR BEGINNING

A project built on care.

Her Care began with the belief that everyone deserves access to the basic products they need to feel comfortable, confident, and cared for.

WHY DID YOU START IT?

Care should be a necessity, not a luxury.

I started Her Care because I believe basic hygiene products should be a necessity, not a luxury. Many girls and women experience situations where they cannot easily afford or access products they need, especially those experiencing financial difficulties or staying in shelters.

WHAT INSPIRED YOU?

Small acts of kindness can make a difference.

I was inspired by the idea that small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone's life. I wanted to create something that could provide practical support while also reminding girls and women that they are cared for, valued, and deserving of dignity.

♡

Every act of care has the potential to make someone feel seen, valued, and supported.

WHO IS INVOLVED?

Meet Our Team

OUR GOALS

What Do You Hope to Accomplish?

Because every girl deserves dignity.

Our goal is to provide hygiene care kits to girls and women in need, particularly through shelters and community organizations. As Her Care grows, we hope to organize larger donation drives, build partnerships with local organizations, reach more people, and create a lasting positive impact in our community.
