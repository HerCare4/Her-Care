Who We Are

Her Care is a community-based passion project focused on making essential hygiene products more accessible to girls and women in need.

OUR STORY

Care should never feel out of reach.

Her Care was started by Ayani Bhakta as a community-based passion project focused on supporting girls and women who may struggle to access basic hygiene products.


I started Her Care because I believe basic hygiene products should be a necessity, not a luxury. Many girls and women experience situations where they cannot easily afford or access products they need, especially those experiencing financial difficulties or staying in shelters.

WHY HER CARE

Small acts can make a difference.

Her Care was inspired by the idea that small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone's life.

♡

I wanted to create something that could provide practical support while also reminding girls and women that they are cared for, valued, and deserving of dignity.

OUR BEGINNING

The Heart Behind Her Care
