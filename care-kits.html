CARE KITS ♡

Small Kits. Meaningful Care.

Every care kit is created to provide essential hygiene products while reminding someone that they are cared for.

WHAT WE PROVIDE ♡

Essentials Provided by HerCare.

HerCare provides essential hygiene products to help girls and women manage their personal care with dignity.

Pads

Essential menstrual products provided to support safe and comfortable period care.

Tampons

Menstrual products provided as part of essential personal hygiene support.

Deodorant

Personal hygiene essentials that help people feel fresh and confident throughout the day.

Vaginal Wipes

Hygiene products provided for personal care and safety.
