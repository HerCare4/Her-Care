♡ WHY IT MATTERS ♡

Everyone Deserves Care & Dignity

Access to basic hygiene products can make an important difference in a person's everyday life.

♡

What Is Period Poverty?

Period poverty is the inability to buy menstrual products, access clean and safe facilities, and receive proper menstrual education.

♡

Access Matters

Menstrual products are essential health necessities that allow women to manage their periods safely, maintain hygiene, and participate in day-to-day life.

♡

Dignity Matters

Having access to basic hygiene products can help people feel supported, prepared, and cared for.

♡

Community Matters

When communities work together, small acts of kindness can help create meaningful change.

♡

How Her Care Helps

Her Care works to provide hygiene care kits to girls and women in need, particularly through shelters and community organizations.

By providing practical products and spreading awareness, we hope to remind every girl and woman that she deserves care, confidence, and dignity.

♡

Care Should Never Be A Luxury

Together, we can help make essential hygiene products more accessible to people in our community.
