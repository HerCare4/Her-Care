ABOUT US

Who is Her Care?

A student-led community project focused on compassion, support, and making a difference.

Who Started Her Care?

Her Care – by Ayani Bhakta was started by Ayani Bhakta as a community-based passion project focused on supporting girls and women who may struggle to access basic hygiene products.

Why Did You Start It?

I started Her Care because I believe basic hygiene products should be a necessity, not a luxury. Many girls and women experience situations where they cannot easily afford or access products they need, especially those experiencing financial difficulties or staying in shelters.

What Inspired You?

I was inspired by the idea that small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone's life. I wanted to create something that could provide practical support while also reminding girls and women that they are cared for, valued, and deserving of dignity.

WHY IT MATTERS

Everyone deserves access to basic care.

🌸

What is Period Poverty?

Period poverty is the inability to buy menstrual products, access clean and safe facilities, and receive proper menstrual education.

💜

Why Products Matter

Menstrual products are essential health necessities that allow women to manage their periods safely, maintain hygiene, and participate in day-to-day life.

🌿

The Challenge

A lack of access to menstrual products can create barriers to school, work, everyday activities, and overall well-being.
