The heart behind HerCare

We are a small team with a big purpose. Together, we work to create change in our community.

Our Team

Our team works together to organize donations, manage finances, create hygiene care kits, spread awareness, and connect with community organizations. While each member has a different role, we all share the same goal: to make basic hygiene products more accessible and remind every girl that she deserves care, confidence, and dignity.
