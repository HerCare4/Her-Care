HerCare ♡
HerCare is a community-based passion project created to support girls and women by providing essential hygiene products while promoting dignity, 
confidence, and care within the community.

Project Purpose
HerCare focuses on helping people who may have difficulty accessing basic hygiene products,
including individuals experiencing financial difficulties or staying in shelters.
HerCare is intended to grow through community involvement, donations, partnerships, and awareness.
